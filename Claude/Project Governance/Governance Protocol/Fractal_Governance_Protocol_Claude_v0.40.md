## 3. The decisions (C-094, C-095)

**C-094 — field testing is the standard proving process (the fieldnotes loop generalized).** Every new development that lands for real use — a release, a tool, a surface, a client — is proven by a **field run**: real use by its intended user, with a pre-canon fieldnotes ledger (C-062) capturing frictions *and* green data at the moment they occur, appended run by run. The ledger's slate is triaged and ingested at the next phase boundary, and fixes land in the **generator** (kernel, next release), never as patches hand-applied to the field instance. One ledger per test subject; the first shipping run's file is the class's founding member and keeps serving the beta under test. The rule costs nothing new — it is the practiced v0.37→v0.38 loop written down so it must keep happening.

**C-095 — remove on observed drag (the compaction discipline; minimal necessary complexity gains its mirror).** The meta-rule has always had an *add* discipline (build on observed need, C-021) and never a *remove* one. Now it does: **living projections compact at phase boundaries** — accumulated narrative that already exists verbatim in the frozen tier (Protocols, Return Packages, Scans) is replaced by pointers to it. Compaction is lossless by construction: derived projections re-tell, they never own; truth lives in canon and frozen history. Trigger: observed drag (orientation or close cost growing without information gain) — the datum that fired this one is recorded at OQ-28 as the *mother's own affordability*, the weight dial's first inside-view measurement. **First exercise, same session:** the Local Context's fifteen accumulated session paragraphs (v0.40–v0.54) became a one-line-per-session pointer table; 8,629 → ~2,300 words; the aspect line brought current with Global v0.4 in the same pass; `check_versions.py` clean on the result. The Register and Index remain candidates at their own observed-drag triggers — compaction is per-document, on drag, never a sweep for its own sake.
