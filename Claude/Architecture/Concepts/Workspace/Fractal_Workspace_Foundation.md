## 0 · What this document is for, and what fired it

The WS domain has been the corpus's declared open part since the clean-era forge (C-051): a route with an address and no document, its vision carried pointer-only by the archive. The forge trigger (OQ-23) was *the first WS build* — and what fired it was not a build but the thing the discipline actually asks for: an **observed need**. On 2026-08-15, with arc 2 live and KNet doing real research, Max stated the requirement of record:

> *"The terminal is not a good work environment — it's not good to read text, and it's inefficient if I consider doing research with papers. I would like a splittable interface where I can read documents, interact with AI agents, or even other advanced tools — that last part I would leave conceptually open."*

This document exists to let the workspace vision mature in one place; to hold the archive's reservoir (Parts VI–VIII) in governed, citable form instead of pointer-only exile; and to give move 2's app exactly one source to project from. **Standing limit on everything below:** entries are *triggers and constraints*, never build orders (the C-086 discipline).

## 1 · The problem — the surface fails the reader

FRACTAL's founding problem is fragmentation; the governed session solved its deepest layer — context continuity across time. But the surface that delivers the governed session is a **scrolling terminal**: a single pane, typographically hostile to sustained reading, with no way to hold a document open *beside* the conversation about it. The first real research instance surfaced this within days of its birth. A researcher working with papers needs, at minimum, to **read one thing while discussing it with another** — and the terminal forces a serial life onto inherently parallel work. This is the same context tax FRACTAL exists to eliminate, re-observed one layer up: not *between sessions* but *within the working hour*.

## 2 · The accepted ground (binding, restated — nothing here is new)

What any workspace build must ride on is already decided, and this document changes none of it:

- **Entry is a query shape** (C-068): axes, prefix descent, weight-ranked landmarks; the default lobby is client configuration, never an entry-point node (that idea is dissolved, per v0.24). What remains for WS is **rendering**: `enter` spatially, `trace` as navigation.
- **The interface is client-side and thin.** The substrate — store, log, tools, close ritual — never moves for a client. A workspace is a *client of the kernel* exactly as Claude Code is, plugging in through the adapter class (C-065).
- **The ontology is settled** (C-017): two shell classes — Galaxy (container, opens a workspace) and Operator (capability, opens an interface). The workspace vision renders this ontology; it does not reopen it.
- **Implementations are tiers** (C-079): the contract is kernel, its renderings are tiers. The terminal ritual is tier 0 of the workspace exactly as grep is tier 0 of navigation — *correct*, not merely adequate, for a newborn; outgrown by observed need, never by fashion.
- **The delivery route is recorded** (v0.37 §5): a FRACTAL-branded desktop app as a thin shell over the Claude Agent SDK — the second of the two distribution routes, and OQ-23's recorded likeliest igniter. So it proved.

## 3 · The vision reservoir — Parts VI–VIII, forged clean

The archive holds a complete spatial-workspace vision (Master Index Parts VI–VIII; Max's UI-vision note), summarized here as *direction*, with the archive remaining the detail reservoir:

- **The spatial workspace.** The main canvas is a local environment of shells — 2D arrangement in the TouchDesigner manner over a 3D spatial atmosphere (Max's note is explicit on this hybrid). Position is personal arrangement, not semantics. One visible layer of depth; the full graph is never rendered at once.
- **Universal Open + wormholes.** One semantic action — *Open* — with one transition language: a Galaxy opens its workspace, an Operator opens its capability interface; the wormhole transition preserves spatial continuity, and the return restores the prior camera.
- **The cave thread.** The visible trail behind the user: retraceable movement, saved research paths, navigation history as an object — the archive's anticipation of what `trace` (C-069) later made contractual.
- **Split-screen and multi-context work** (Part VII ch. 27 — *the oldest form of today's requirement*). Max's note, 2026 spring: roam the project spatially while a doc viewer shows the assembled readable document, **and the two stay coupled** — navigate to a node and the document jumps to the right place, *reversibly*. The note's own condition: *"with our simple credo and the idea of an integrated ID system this might be possible."*
- **Dense analytical interfaces** (ch. 28 — the archive's own honesty): 3D is for navigation, **not every analytical task**; reading, tables, charts, notebooks live in embedded 2D panels. Knowing when to leave the spatial canvas is part of the design.
- **The capability architecture** (Part VIII): no universal content format — a universal integration *contract*; operators host capabilities (native, third-party, existing-software adapters, computational environments) behind typed ports, exchanging references, never unnecessary copies.

## 4 · The near shape — the splittable workbench

Today's requirement and the archive's reservoir agree on a first shape that is far smaller than the Galaxy: a **workbench of coupled panes**, with **the agent at its core**. The pane roster is taken from the proven surface — Max's design directive of record: *replicate the Claude Code client's capabilities; they are a proven concept and already provide the guideline.* Named there and adopted here as the first interfaces:

- **The folder system** — the instance's files as a navigable tree (roots switchable: the governing repo, an instance beside it).
- **A reading surface** — documents rendered for sustained reading: the corpus's own artifacts and, first-class, **research PDFs** with normal viewer interaction. Its USP, stated by Max at the forge: **the agent can track markings** — a passage marked in the reader is recorded where the session's agent can read it, and can be handed directly into the session prompt.
- **A session surface** — the governed AI conversation, the product's engine; the workbench gives it a room rather than a pipe. At tier 0 this is the terminal running the client; a chat-native agent UI is the Agent SDK step behind it.
- **A browser** — the open slot's first occupant, for web sources beside the work.
- **The open slot itself** — "other advanced tools," **deliberately unshaped by Max's instruction.** The archive's operator architecture (§3, last bullet) is the reservoir it will eventually draw from.

Panes follow the tiering pattern (C-079): each is a component with a contract, and the set is open. **Access nests in the homepage** (Max's call at the first slice): the site is the gate, the workbench a room behind it — the served landing is the FRACTAL page and the workbench mounts inside, which is the root-galaxy entry idea (Part VI ch. 24) in its smallest honest form. The workbench is *not* the Galaxy — it is the dense-interface half of the archive vision (ch. 28) shipped first, because that is where the observed need is. The spatial half remains vision behind it, unbetrayed: a workbench pane is exactly what a Galaxy workspace would open for dense work anyway, so building the workbench first builds *toward* the spatial environment, not past it.

## 5 · The coupling — identity is the synchronizer

The load-bearing idea, and the reason this is a FRACTAL workbench rather than a generic split-screen: **panes are coupled through identity, not through the UI.** A selection anywhere is an identity — a `DOC-…`, a node, an event — and every pane interprets the same identity in its own medium: the reading surface scrolls to it, the session surface can be asked about it, a future spatial pane would fly to it. The archive's condition (*"an integrated ID system might make this possible"*) is **now satisfied fact**: identities are minted (C-041/C-042), aliases resolve (C-045), navigation is contracted (`resolve`/`enter`/`trace`/`history`, C-068–C-070). The reversible doc-viewer coupling of Max's note is, in contract terms, *two renderings of one `resolve`* — which is why the workbench needs no invention below the surface, only rendering above it. **Constraint fixed now, cheap:** pane coupling speaks the Navigation Contract's verbs and nothing else — a pane that needs a new verb is proposing a Contract change, which is a kernel decision, not a UI decision.

## 6 · The vehicle — the branded app

The recorded two-route distribution (v0.37 §5) carries this: the developer route (clone + `claude`) stays tier 0 and complete; the **FRACTAL-branded desktop app** — a thin shell over the Claude Agent SDK, plugging in via the adapter class — is the workbench's natural body, serving simultaneously as Max's own research environment (the engine need that fired this document) and move 2's public face (the exterior need it leans onto). The ChatGPT-era technology preferences (Electron over Tauri; React/TypeScript/Three.js as working direction; web-compatible core) are **recorded preferences, provisional then and provisional now** — re-decided by ordinary decision at the first build slice, not inherited as commitments. Global §2 already names *"a working interactive interface"* as the example of the next realisation transition; this is the road to it, walked at trigger pace.

**First observed-use datum (2026-08-16, thirteenth session, per Protocol v0.40) — the verdict the vehicle decision must consume:** the workbench's first real use returned negative — Max: *"it feels more like a 3D printed toy - quick and functional but in the end no real use."* His stated preference in the same breath: **AI agents in desktop apps are an environment he likes working in** — strong evidence for the branded-desktop-app route over further web-thin investment, and the reason the reading work moved to a second client rather than waiting on a rebuild (the second-client trial, fieldnotes entries 30–34). Consequence held deliberately: the walking skeleton is *not* extended until the vehicle is decided — effort at FRACTAL grade was priced by Max as exhausting his limits before research, so the vehicle decision now owns both the quality bar and the build budget question.

## 7 · What this constrains today

Back-propagation, all of it cheap: the workbench's panes must speak the Navigation Contract (§5 — armed as a design tripwire, satisfiable free by building on `resolve`); the reading surface wants the store's artifacts renderable, which they already are (Markdown, C-022); the session surface wants the governed loop embeddable, which the Agent SDK route provides by design; the open slot wants nothing until named. **The first build slice landed in the forge session itself** (`Workbench/` — a thin local web app: the five panes above in walking-skeleton form, resolve-coupled, markings working, nested behind the site at `/`): it enters Architecture State §4 by ordinary decision at its landing, and this document's §4 compresses toward pointers as §4-the-map gains the accepted pieces. Its recorded boundaries: working data (markings) stays out of the repo (gitignored — promotion into an instance is the researcher's act, C-062's logic); dependencies are environment, not knowledge; and the slice is a **client** — the store is never written from a pane.
